Evanescence

Evanescence is an American rock band formed in 1995. Known for their ethereal sound and powerful vocals, they've created hits like "Bring Me to Life" and "My Immortal." The band's music blends gothic rock, alternative metal, and classical elements to create a unique and haunting sound that resonates with fans worldwide.
