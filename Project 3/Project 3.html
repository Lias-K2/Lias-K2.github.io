Evanescence

Evanescence is an American rock band formed in 1995. Known for their ethereal sound and powerful vocals, they've created hits like "Bring Me to Life" and "My Immortal." The band's music blends gothic rock, alternative metal, and classical elements to create a unique and haunting sound that resonates with fans worldwide.

Foo Fighters

Formed in 1994 by Dave Grohl after the breakup of Nirvana, Foo Fighters have become one of the most successful rock bands of the modern era. With hits like "Everlong," "Learn to Fly," and "The Pretender," they've won 12 Grammy Awards and are known for their energetic live performances and melodic rock sound.
